9. 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] Quotations & Citations</span>** (`<blockquote>`, `<q>`, `<cite>`, `<abbr>`)
10. 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] Preformatted & Code-Related Text</span>** (`<pre>`, `<code>`, `<kbd>`, `<samp>`, `<var>`)

12. **<span style="color: #6366f1;">Links & Anchors</span>** (href, target, relative vs absolute, 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] `rel` attribute values</span>** like nofollow/noopener)
13. **<span style="color: #f43f5e;">Line Breaks, Horizontal Rules & Whitespace Handling</span>**

## 🖼️ Module 3: <span style="color: #8b5cf6;">Media & Embedded Content</span>
14. **<span style="color: #ec4899;">Images</span>** (`<img>`, alt text, srcset)
15. 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] `<picture>` Element & Art Direction</span>** (responsive image switching)
16. 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] `<figure>` & `<figcaption>`</span>**
17. **<span style="color: #f59e0b;">Audio & Video</span>** (`<audio>`, `<video>`, controls, sources, tracks/captions)

22. 🆕 **<span style="color: #ef4444;">[NEW] Deprecated/Obsolete Elements & Legacy HTML Awareness</span>** (`<font>`, `<center>`, why avoid them)

## 📊 Module 5: <span style="color: #8b5cf6;">Tables</span>
